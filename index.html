What goes into our builds

01 / Tank

Tanks sized for the route.

  • 2,000 and 2,500-gallon tanks across current inventory
  • Carbon steel and polished aluminum builds
  • Exclusive dealer for APEX Tank commercial vacuum tank units

02 / Vacuum pump

Pump packages from proven names.

  • Battioni, Masport and NVE pump & blower partners
  • 350–393 CFM pumps on the featured trucks
  • Two-year manufacturer warranty on every pump & blower package

03 / Valves & fittings

Brass where it counts.

  • 3″ intake and 4″ discharge valves on the featured trucks
  • Brass lever valves and a 3″ rear sight glass on builds like the 2023 MV607
  • 36″ aluminum toolboxes on most listed trucks

04 / Chassis

The makes fleets already trust.

  • Peterbilt, International, Kenworth and Freightliner
  • Cummins and Paccar diesel engines
  • Automatic transmissions, most of them Allison
